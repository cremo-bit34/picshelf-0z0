Privacy Policy

1. Complete On-Device Autonomy

PicShelf was architected with a fundamental privacy premise: your personal memories belong strictly to you. All tagging, indexing, search embeddings, and photo metadata generation occur directly within your device's browser memory and sandboxed local cache.

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking

We do not track, profile, monetize, or transmit your photo contents to any external servers, machine learning training clusters, or advertising networks.

3. Secure Payments Powered by Google Play

When you subscribe to PicShelf Pro, payment processing is encrypted and managed directly via Google Play Store Billing. We do not store credit card credentials, CVV codes, or UPI pins.

4. Data Portability & Sovereignty

You maintain 100% sovereignty over your data. At any time, you can export a complete JSON archive of all your albums, tags, and organized memories via the Storage & Data Management panel.

PRIVACY & DATA SAFETY

PicShelf Privacy Policy

🔒 100% On-Device Privacy Architecture: All your photos, albums, edits, and AI subject segmentation run purely on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud database.

1. Permissions & Data Access

• Photos & Storage: Required to display, organize, compress, duplicate-detect, and edit photos on your phone.
• Location Metadata: Used to display photo capture places on the built-in world map.
• Camera: Allows taking new photos directly into custom albums.
• Google Play Billing: Securely processes in-app subscriptions and lifetime purchases through Google Play.

2. Zero Tracking & Advertising

PicShelf does not collect, sell, or rent your personal information. There are no tracking cookies, analytics SDKs, or third-party advertising brokers.

3. Data Deletion

You can wipe all app data and cache at any time via Android App Info > Clear Storage or by uninstalling the app.
