EDITORIAL PLEDGE

Privacy Policy

1. Complete On-Device Autonomy

Aura Photos was architected with a fundamental privacy premise: your personal memories belong strictly to you. All tagging, indexing, search embeddings, and photo metadata generation occur directly within your device's browser memory and sandboxed local cache.

2. Zero Third-Party Tracking

We do not track, profile, monetize, or transmit your photo contents to any external servers, machine learning training clusters, or advertising networks.

3. Secure Payments Powered by Razorpay

When you subscribe to Aura Pro, payment processing is encrypted with bank-grade 256-bit SSL encryption via Razorpay. We do not store credit card credentials, CVV codes, or UPI pins.

4. Data Portability & Sovereignty

You maintain 100% sovereignty over your data. At any time, you can export a complete JSON archive of all your albums, tags, and organized memories via the Storage & Data Management panel.
